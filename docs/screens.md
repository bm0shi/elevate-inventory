# Screens and front-end files

The full notes for each front-end file: what it draws, where its data comes
from, and the owner's decisions behind it. `CLAUDE.md` has the one-line version.
Read the section before changing the file.

## dash2.js — the Admin dashboard

The Admin dashboard (the "new look", default since the owner made it permanent). The classic dashboard in `index.html` is the backup: the sidebar's "Classic view" switches a device to it (localStorage `dashView='classic'`), and "Back to the new look" switches back; if dash2.js fails to load, Classic shows for that visit. Served at `/dash2.js`, loaded only when switched on; its history series come from `/api/dash2` (owner). Its gross sales come from `inv_sales_daily` (`/api/sales/history`; orders per day too), counted like Seller Central's "Ordered product sales" (`orderRecords` in `lib/velocity.js`): cancelled lines, $0 lines on non-pending orders (free replacements), `is-replacement-order` and Non-Amazon (MCF) orders are left out; pending lines with no price yet take the ASIN's average. The sales panel: periods Today / 7 / 30 / MTD / YTD / Custom, and under them Gross sales, Orders, Units, Avg order, Avg unit price, Refunds, Amazon fees, Returns, each against the period before; refunds/fees come on time from the Finances API's financial events (`lib/finevents.js`, table `inv_fin_daily`; pulled each morning for 60 days, by Update today for today + yesterday, and with the full history), falling back to settlements on days the events don't cover; refunds, fees and returns are compared as a rate and only over days covered in both periods. Under the metrics, "🏆 Top 50 sellers" (a drop-down, also opened by each period card's "Top 50 ▾") lists the period's 50 best sellers by units, numbered (#) by a worth-reordering rank: profit per day = net profit ÷ days covered (units per day × profit per unit; `scoreRows`), each name with its ASIN (click to copy, owner 10/23), (the Overview's "Top sellers" card shows the same period's best 8 by units with a "See all" button that opens it, `paintTopCard`; it used to stay on velocity's last 60 days, owner 10/26), with net profit, margin (profit ÷ sales), profit/day, "vs before" (profit/day against the same-length stretch just before, same costs and Amazon rates, `prevRange`/`trend`; not for Today) and ROI on cost (profit per unit ÷ landed cost per unit, so over 100% when profit beats cost) as columns, all sortable; "Ranked: N of 50" says how many have no cost (—) and so aren't ranked; data only, no suggestions; from `/api/finance/top-sellers` (owner; `lib/top-profit.js`: the period's sales − what Amazon keeps, split the way Amazon charges it: referral, refunds, promotions and digital services as each listing's own % of price from 6 months of settlements (so a price change moves the referral fee with it), plus FBA and other flat fees per unit from the last 60 days (else 6 months), or the store-wide share of sales when a listing has under 5 settled units (shown ≈) − landed cost per unit as in Product Profit; no product cost → no number; tap a row for its per-unit breakdown: price, Amazon's cut, product cost (a duo's bottles each with its cost, or "bought as a set"), supplies, labor, freight) over `inv_sales_asin_daily` (units and sales per listing per Pacific day, written by `saveSalesDays` from the same order pulls; the days a pull covers are replaced whole). It says when a period reaches back before the first saved day. Update today and Pull full history show a progress card the moment they're pressed: the server's named steps (`salesJob.steps`/`step`), a bar that fills by step (each weighted by its typical time, "N of M months" exactly, never 100% until done), what Amazon is doing, a timer, and a green done / red failed line at the end; it also appears for an update already running (another tab, the auto update on load). The bar is a PIXEL STREET (owner 11/14, "a cool interactive loading bar … something cool and fun"; a runner above the bar, then inside a thin bar, "got a little worse"; then a reference, a chunky pill slider filled with a pixel-art scene, day on one side of a glass knob and night on the other): a 56px pill (`.d2-lane`, `--p` = how far it has got) showing a pixel-art street drawn in dash2.js as rectangles (`PXR`, `pxTile`, `pxWarehouse`, `pxHub`, `pxOrb`, palettes `PAL.day` / `PAL.night`): our warehouse at the left end, a plain blue Amazon building with a coin sign at the right (no logo). What hasn't loaded is night (`.d2-sky.night`), what has is day (`.d2-sky.day`, clipped to the edge); a glass lens with a spinning dollar coin rides the edge (`.d2-lens.d2-coin`) and our pixel delivery truck drives right behind it (`.d2-truck`, `chaseHtml`). Tapping the truck makes it hop, tapping the lens pays out (`chaseTap`); a finished update is all day with the truck at Amazon and a burst; a failed one keeps the plain red bar. One sun and one moon are their own layer (in the repeating tile there were five suns). Our own drawings; not the Monopoly man, who is Hasbro's, lookalikes included, though the owner asked twice. The whole lane is `data-nodark` (the scene's colours are the picture). The card is compact (`.d2-pullbar.mini`: title and status on one line). The running card is built once and then only its numbers move (`paintPull` used to redraw it every second, which would restart the animation). Stops under Reduce Motion.

## onhand2.js — On Hand

On Hand's new look (default; Classic is the backup per device, localStorage `ohView='classic'`, via the "Classic view" link / the classic "✨ New look" button). `renderOnhand` in index.html still filters and sorts (the classic controls stay in the page, hidden, and the new toolbar drives them); this file only draws: header + sync pill, tiles that filter, sticky toolbar, cards or a table. Each product shows a GRAND TOTAL (owner 10/24, `grandTotal`): on hand (pending prep and prepped included) + on the way + at Amazon, the same parts as the stock bar; a column in the table, a "Total" cell on the cards. Under the stats, one "Sells as" table per card (`sellsAs`, owner 11/5: "hard to read what items I can make into duos … too clustered"; it replaced "Pairs into duos" and "At Amazon by listing", which repeated the duos): a row per listing, SINGLE then each DUO (red pill, tinted), the same columns all the way down (We sold 30d = our own sales, `/api/our-sold30?to=today`; 3P avg `ss3p`; At Amazon; On the way; Total; FBA cover), and under each duo "Made from A this bottle N free + B partner N free = can build N duos" (free = on hand less pending prep and prepped); duo ASIN, FNSKU and duos/case ✎ under its name; on a phone each row stacks into labeled boxes (`data-l`). Styled as one calm table (owner 11/5: "make it look professional like a multi million dollar company"): `.sa-*` classes only (the first version reused `.oh2-pill`, the header's sync pill, and the page's dark table header leaked in; `.oh2 .sa th` overrides it), light uppercase header, every number in one ink colour with tabular figures (only FBA cover keeps its colour badge), a three-line listing cell (kind tag, name without the dotted link line, ASIN / FNSKU / case), and the duo's parts as tiles: A, B, then a green "Can build N" or red "Can't build 0 · part B is short". The 3P cell left the stats row (it's per listing in the table). Every action calls the same function as Classic, so nothing about stock changes. Served at `/onhand2.js`.

## look2.css — the new look

The new look for every other screen (Pending Prep, Prepped & Ready, Shipment Production, Ship to FBA, Order Check-In, Receiving, Activity, Admin): slim top bar, one nav row that sticks, page heads, and one style for cards, buttons, inputs and tables. Styling only, laid over the existing markup under `html.nl` (set in index.html's head unless this device chose Classic, localStorage `appLook='classic'`); every button calls the same function as before. The header's "Classic view" / "✨ New look" is one switch for the whole app (it also sets `ohView` and `dashView`). Element rules skip `.oh2` (On Hand), `.d2` (dashboard) and `.pal` (search), which style their own. Toolbars that stick use `stickyTop()`. Pages run up to 1920px wide with a 20px margin (`html.nl .panel`; owner 11/5: 1480px left wide empty sides).

## look3.css, theme3.js — the Bold look and dark mode

The BOLD look (owner 11/13, picked from three previews: "fresh, clean, modern but useful as it is now", with a light and a dark mode): big numbers, colour-blocked stat tiles (teal warehouse, blue Amazon, purple sales), round cards with no hairlines, pill buttons (gradient = do it / the next step, card-white = secondary, red = removes stock), pill nav on frosted glass. A third look, laid OVER the new one, and the DEFAULT for every device since 11/14 (owner: "make the new bold look the default setting for everyone"): the head adds `html.n3` beside `html.nl` and loads look3.css after look2.css (so every look2 rule still applies) unless this device chose "Standard look" (localStorage `appLook='standard'`) or "Classic view" (`'classic'`); the old value `'new'` only ever meant "not Classic", so it gets Bold too. `setAppLook(v)` stores `'classic'`, `'standard'` or `'bold'`; the standard look's header keeps a "✨ Bold look" button to come back. Styling only, like look2: nothing is moved, every button calls the same function. look3.css retunes look2's `--k-*` tokens and adds its own `--b-*`; everything theme-dependent in it is a `var()`. DARK ("☾ Dark" / "☀ Light" in the header, `appDark='1'`, `html.n3.dk`, `setAppDark`): look3.css carries the dark tokens, and theme3.js turns dark the colours the app writes outside any token (3,500 inline styles in index.html, the CSS that onhand2.js / dash2.js / palette.js inject, SVG `fill` / `stroke`) by their job: a light background → the dark card family (a tint keeps its hue), dark text → light (coloured text keeps its hue), a light border → a faint line; saturated mid-tones (buttons, status, the orange mark) and dark text on its own bright background are left alone. It skips look3.css, and anything inside `data-nodark` (a drawing whose colours are the picture: the sales bar's pixel street; turned dark, an earlier version's coin lost its $). An element's `style=""` is never rewritten through `el.style` (the browser re-spells the attribute and look2 / look3 recognise buttons by its exact text, `button.action[style*="background:#fff;"]`; the first try turned "Send back to On Hand" into a primary button): the dark colours are appended after the original text and light mode puts the text back; stylesheet rules are changed in place and remembered. A MutationObserver covers re-renders; switching is live (no reload); printing goes back to light for the print (`beforeprint` / `afterprint`). Served at `/look3.css` and `/theme3.js`, in sw.js's saved files. When a screen looks off in dark, fix the colour's ROLE in theme3.js's `MAP` or give look3.css a rule with a token; don't add per-screen dark colours to index.html.

## palette.js — search everything (⌘K)

Search everything: ⌘K / Ctrl+K anywhere, `/` when not typing (On Hand's new look keeps `/` for its own search), or the header's Search button. Products (name, ASIN, SKU, FNSKU, a scanned barcode; ranked by `lib/search.js`, on-the-shelf first), FBA shipments, app-made plans, Cosmoprof invoices (order #, item #, description) and screens (Admin's once unlocked). Results from `/api/search` (`auth`, no money). Picking one only navigates, through the screens' own functions. A question (or 2+ words, once Admin is unlocked) offers "Ask: …", which opens Ask Elevate in the same box (a thread with follow-ups; Esc goes back to search). Recent picks per device (localStorage `palRecent`). Works in both looks.

## ask.js — Ask Elevate

Ask Elevate: the owner types a question in the ⌘K box ("how much profit is the tea tree liter shampoo?") and Claude (`claude-opus-5-5`, `@anthropic-ai/sdk` tool runner, server-side refusal fallback) answers from the app's data. Read-only tools: `find_products`, `product_profit` and `monthly_pnl` (through the app's own `/api/finance/*` routes, so answers match the screens), and `run_sql` (one SELECT, checked by `lib/ask-sql.js`, run in a READ ONLY transaction with an 8 s timeout and a 200-row cap). `POST /api/ask` (`ownerAuth`) streams NDJSON progress steps, then the answer and its cost. Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; without it the box says so. Mounted at the bottom of server.js.

## repricer.js — the night price drop

The night price drop, brought in from the owner's separate amazon-pricing bot (Admin → Prices → Repricer, `ownerAuth`). An on/off switch (arrives OFF), a flat drop ($) with an optional different drop per ASIN (`overrides`; everything not listed takes the main drop, the % cap still applies; set on the Repricer screen or by ticking products on Admin → FBA Inventory and "Send to repricer", which saves only the list), and up to 6 time slots in Arizona time (each 15 min–12 h, may cross midnight, switched-on slots can't overlap); settings in `inv_cache` `reprice_settings`, rules in `lib/reprice.js`. During a slot, every FBA listing with stock where someone else holds the Buy Box drops by the amount (skips: no stock, an unrestored earlier drop, not buyable, a sale price, over the % cap, a Buy Box check error, the never-drop SKUs); the price to put back is saved in `inv_reprice` BEFORE the price changes. Drops stop 5 min before the slot ends. Restores always run, also when switched off: every pending row except the running slot's goes back on the next minute (so switching off puts prices back), a failed one is retried with a growing wait and alerts at the 3rd failure; a drop older than 24 h is only put back if Amazon still shows the drop price. The drop per slot per night is claimed atomically in `inv_cache` (`reprice_drop`). Amazon calls in `spapi.js`: `getListingPrice`, `setListingPrice` (Listings PATCH of `purchasable_offer`, a non-ACCEPTED answer is a failure), `ownsBuyBox`; they need our seller id (`SELLER_ID`, else `AMAZON_SELLER_ID(_FM)`, else learned from offers). Only one repricer may run: the old bot must be stopped before this one is switched on. The screen also shows each slot, the next drop and the put-back time in California and New York time beside Arizona's (`RP_ZONES`, `rpSlotZones`, `rpTimeZones`; owner 11/14, "time changed for the rest of the nation but AZ stayed the same"): Arizona is always UTC-7 and the other two are worked out for the day, so the gap follows daylight saving by itself. Display only; the slots are still set and run in Arizona time.

## sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icons/ — the home-screen app

Install to the home screen as an app (PWA, owner 11/3): the manifest (name "Elevate", standalone, dark teal), PNG icons (an amber "E" tile like the header mark; an iPhone can't use an SVG/emoji icon), and a service worker served by server.js with `BUILD_ID` filled in (`__BUILD__`), so each deploy installs a new one and deletes the old cache. It never handles `/api/*` (stock and money always come live); the page and the app's own files open from the copy saved for that build (cache-first; owner 11/4: network-first made the home-screen app feel slower than Chrome), so the app opens at once and with no Wi-Fi; a deploy's new worker saves the new files and the page shows "✨ New version ready — tap to update" (`swUpdate`), reloading by itself the next time it's hidden. Registered in index.html's head on https/localhost. The page also knows its own build (server.js stamps `__PAGE_BUILD__` when serving `/`) and checks `/api/ping` a few seconds after opening, on coming back to the app and every 5 min; a different build shows the same bar (owner 11/4: the bar never showed, since the browser checks for a new worker only now and then and a home-screen app resumed from the background never reloads); the tap deletes the `elevate-*` caches and reloads. Opened and not touched yet (15 s, no tap/key): it reloads to the new build by itself, once per build (sessionStorage `swAuto`). A refresh (F5, the navigation's `cache` is `no-cache`) gets the live page from the server (`freshPage` in sw.js, 4 s then the saved copy), and if it's another build the worker drops its saved files so the scripts come live too (owner 11/4: F5 in the installed Chrome app only showed the bar over the old page). `controllerchange` re-checks the build instead of offering blindly (it showed the bar on a page a refresh had already loaded new). The server compresses its responses (`compression`, pinned; index.html ~1 MB → ~290 KB, data lists 70-90% smaller), except Ask Elevate's NDJSON stream.

## tablet.js — tablet mode

Tablet mode for the floor, per device (header's "📱 Tablet", localStorage `tabletMode='1'`, `html.tablet` set in the head): big touch targets and scan box, big messages, screen kept awake (Wake Lock), an Online/Offline pill that checks `/api/ping` (no login, returns only the build) and counts scans saved by the offline queue, and the active screen's scan box keeps the cursor with the on-screen keyboard down (`inputmode=none`) until ⌨ is tapped. Works in both looks; scans go through the same handlers.
