# The long rules

Domain rules learned the hard way, in full: what the owner asked for, the incident
behind it, and the functions that carry it. `CLAUDE.md` has a one-line version of
each. Read the rule before changing the code it covers, and keep it current.

## Money

- **Stock at cost** (owner 10/27): beside every retail value of the stock (FBA Inventory totals, the dashboard's Total retail value and its Available / Pending prep / Prepped / In transit / At FBA legend) the same units at what they cost us: `/api/stock-costs` (`ownerAuth`) gives each listing's product cost (`avg_cost`, else `unit_cost`; a duo with none of its own is its bottles added up), and `fbaRowValues` adds `*Cost` / `costTotal`. A listing with units but no cost is counted in `costMissing` and said, never added as $0; that line opens a list of them with a cost box each (`stockCostMissingHtml` / `saveStockCost`, the same manual cost as Data Sources → Enter missing costs; a duo shows a box per bottle without a cost, `missingBottles`). A listing at Amazon that isn't in our catalog has no `inv_products` row, so its typed cost lives only in `inv_cost_history`; `/api/stock-costs` blends those lots directly (the box used to come back empty, owner 10/27). finance.js `productInfo` does the same for every money screen (P&L COGS, Product Profit, Top 50; names from the FBA Inventory pull, `amazonNames`), Data Sources' missing costs lists sold listings that aren't in the catalog, and each Top 50 row with no cost has a "+ add cost" button in every period (owner 10/29). The P&L's incomplete-months banner names each line that isn't complete, why, and that line's own fix (`finGoFix`). Product cost only, not supplies, labor or freight.

- **P&L is current, settlements correct it** (owner 10/29: the P&L stopped at the last payout, $112k vs the dashboard's $149k). `pullFinEvents` also saves the financial events line by line, settlement-style (`lib/finevents.js` `eventLines`, table `inv_fin_event_lines`, last ~90 days). finance.js `aggregate` adds the lines Amazon hasn't settled (`unsettled`): an order's lines until a settlement carries that order id (order and refund told apart), lines with no order only after the last settled day, nothing from orders 30+ days before it. Shown as "incl. $X est." under Sales plus a blue banner; the morning sync now also starts the settlement sync (`startSettlementSync`), so each payout replaces its estimate within a day. Top 50's Amazon rates stay settled-only (`settledOnly`). Undated service fees (storage) only arrive with the settlement. The lines are written under an advisory lock (Update today and a 60-day pull at once doubled the overlap); a start with under 30 days saved reads the last 60 (the table began empty and Update today reads two days, so October's estimate had only 10/28–29); the P&L names any days after the last settlement the events don't reach (`estimate.gap`), and its "Day-by-day check" (`estimate.check`, `finEstCheckHtml`) lists per day the dashboard's ordered sales, settled sales, money-event sales and what the estimate took, with when the events were last read (`inv_cache` `fin_event_lines`, a failure included). Under the statement, "Why Sales here differ from the dashboard" (`monthPnl` `bridge`, owner 11/4) goes from the dashboard's ordered sales (`inv_sales_daily`, ordered day, full price) to the P&L's: − not charged this month (shipped later, cancelled, not in the money data yet, less last month's orders charged now) = item sales charged (`t.principal`) + shipping & gift wrap − refunds − promotions = P&L Sales. The statement itself shows Gross sales, Refunds, Promotions & coupons and Net sales as separate lines (owner 11/4: one "Sales, less refunds" line hid where the gap to Amazon's $161,745 went).

- **Loan payments** (owner 10/30: the Amazon loan, $11,757.62 a month from the bank, "deduct the amount so we know how much money is left over"): a Rent & overhead item with category "Loan payment" (any category with "loan") is its own P&L line, "Amazon loan payment", under Royalty, not overhead (`overheadFor` returns `loan` / `loanItems`); net takes it off and reads "NET (WHAT'S LEFT OVER)" while any month has one. The whole payment, by the owner's choice (only the interest is an expense to an accountant). Cash & Draws' operating buffer includes it.

## Stock and prep

- **Pending Prep can't go past the shelf:** adding a job, or raising one with `/api/pending-prep/set`, is refused (`notEnough`) when it asks for more than is free: on hand less what's prepped and already in Pending Prep (duo jobs and duos count their bottles), under a per-bottle advisory lock. Duos by `duoShortfall`, singles by `singleShortfall` (owner 10/22: Detangler took a 60 job with 36 on the shelf, because only duos were checked). A duo card's bottle lines show "N available", counted as On Hand counts it (on hand less pending prep and prepped, `components[].available`); they showed the raw total, 999 while On Hand said 0 (owner 10/31).

- **FBM orders come off warehouse stock** (owner 11/4, "go back one week and remove it from anywhere"): every orders pull (morning sync, Update today, full pull; `orderRecords` marks `fbm` = fulfillment-channel Merchant and `shipped`) runs `applyFbmOrders`: each SHIPPED FBM line since `inv_cache` `fbm_since` (set once at first start to a week back) is taken off On Hand once (`inv_fbm_orders`, primary key order + SKU + ASIN, written in the same transaction), under the per-bottle locks, where it disturbs the least (`lib/fbm.js` `planTake`): free shelf stock, then pre-labeled, then Pending Prep jobs of that listing (pre-label jobs, next batch, open, a claimed job last), then Prepped & Ready; a duo comes off as its bottles. A unit found nowhere, or a listing not in the catalog, is NOT taken (no count below zero) and is logged as an Activity note for a hand fix. Activity shows the "📦 FBM orders taken off stock" box (`/api/fbm/recent`, `auth`, units only; "Check now" = `/api/fbm/check`, reads Amazon at once). On Hand shows a small "−3 FBM 7d" marker on each product with units taken off in the last 7 days (`fbmMark`, `/api/fbm/marks`: bottles from the Activity lines, a duo under its own ASIN); tapping it opens Activity. The same marker is on Prepped & Ready's cards and its PRE-LABELED SKUS table, and a one-line "📦 FBM orders" status heads Prepped & Ready (`renderPrepFbm`: units taken off in 7 days, how many from pre-labeled / Prepped & Ready, when Amazon was last read, Check now; owner 11/4 "I don't see FBM stock removal anywhere here"). Unshipped FBM orders wait, since they can still be cancelled.

- **Send back to On Hand** (owner 11/5, "in case I screw up … easier than editing 10 SKUs"): Pending Prep's "↩ Send back to On Hand…" turns on picking (a red "Send back" tick on each job card and list row, `data-ppid`; a sticky bar with the count, Select all showing, Send N back, Cancel; `_ppSel`, `ppSelPaint`). `POST /api/pending-prep/remove` (`auth`, `{ids}`) deletes the picked jobs in one transaction: no stock moves (a job only sets bottles aside, so they're Available again), anyone still on a removed job is signed off (`inv_prep_crew.left_at`), and each removal is an Activity note with qty 0 (so the stock audit doesn't count it). A job already gone is just not there, so a repeat is harmless.

- **Duo items vs Duos I can build** (owner 11/5, "not matching up"): Duo items = every bottle that goes into a duo, stock or not; Duos I can build = every duo with both bottles free now (`duoBuildInfo`). Duo items carries a line saying so with the same count (`duoItemsNote`) and a button to the builder; the builder says "N of your M duos". `/api/bundles` returns every duo, its own listing in the catalog or not (it JOINed the duo's listing, so a duo without one dropped out of the builder while the bottle cards still listed it; such a duo is named from the FBA inventory pull, else "Duo <ASIN>", `in_catalog` false).

- **Prelabeling only (owner 10/20):** labelling with no shipment planned, so the floor has work while waiting for inbound shipments. On Hand's "Prelabeling only" button (next to Prep) adds an `inv_pending_prep.prelabel` job: its own job (merges only with another pre-label job, never with a shipment job or the next batch), shown on Pending Prep under ITEMS NEEDING LABEL (NOT PART OF ANY SHIPMENT), can't be ticked into a shipment, counted as spoken-for stock but not in FBA capacity or "units to ship". Finishing it (or a scan, after the product's shipment jobs are filled) adds to `inv_prepped` as usual and to `inv_prepped.prelabel_qty`, the pre-labelled part of that row. `/api/prep/list` returns `items` (qty − prelabel_qty, what shipments and ticks read) and `prelabeled` (PRE-LABELED SKUS on Prepped & Ready); "Ready to ship" (`/api/prep/prelabel-move`) moves units up, no stock moves. A trigger (`inv_prepped_clamp`) keeps prelabel_qty ≤ qty, so shipping and other take-offs use the ready units first; a scan's undo takes its pre-labelled part off (`pre` on the undo op).

## Sales and demand figures

- **Our own sales are never SmartScout's guess** (owner 10/23: Tea Tree Leave-In 33.8 showed — in US with 25 sold at Amazon; SmartScout's file for us left it out). The US column on Smart Scout Orders and "your units" on the Attack list are our real units from `inv_sales_asin_daily` over 30 days (SmartScout's figures are "estimated 30 day"), ending on the day of the latest SmartScout seller upload (`ourLast30`); uploading a seller file also starts the quick sales pull so those days are fresh. SmartScout's estimate of us (`ssus`) is no longer a forecast signal. AVG is still the other sellers only. Pending Prep (list view and Next batch) shows "You sold (30d)" from `/api/our-sold30` (`auth`, an owner-approved exception) beside the 3P average, with each other seller's figure under it (`/api/ss-avg` `by`, e.g. SB 89 · SD 74).

- **How it sells, on Pending Prep and Prepped & Ready** (owner 11/4, "something condensed"): `sellStrip(asin)` on Pending Prep cards and Prepped cards, and sortable columns in the Pre-labeled table: Sold 30d (ours, the real last 30 days to today: `/api/our-sold30?to=today`; the plain call's window ends on the last Smart Scout upload and read weeks old here, owner 11/4), In stock (days with stock available at Amazon of the last 30 snapshot days, `inv_fba_daily`, from the same `?to=today` call: a listing that was out can't sell), 3P avg (what one third-party seller sells a month counting EVERY 3P seller on the listing, owner 11/4: the pie ÷ the 3P seller count, `/api/ss-avg` `all`, `allSellersAvg` in `lib/smartscout.js`: SmartScout's listing units and FBA sellers, else Keepa's monthly sold and 3P Buy Box winners; the seller-file average, which covered only the 3 sellers we have files for and read ~0 for an offer that doesn't move, is only the fallback; `ss3p` in index.html, also on Pending Prep's 3P avg columns), At Amazon (+ on the way), Days (that stock at the best demand estimate, `demandRate`; red under 21, amber under 45) and Rank (Keepa sales rank from `/api/products`). Same data and auth as Pending Prep's list view; `sellRepaint` redraws Prepped & Ready as each arrives. The strip is a full-width row of equal boxes under the card's top row, not inside the name column, and a single's case badge wraps inside a narrow qty column (owner 11/4: a pre-label card ran ~770 px tall with the name one word a line, "need to be boxes"). Prepped & Ready also has an FBA capacity gauge (`renderPrepCap`, owner 11/4: pre-labeled stock never goes through Pending Prep, so "how big a shipment can I build"): room left (limit − at Amazon + on the way) − Prepped & Ready − Pending Prep = cu ft free, and how many pre-labeled units fit at their own average size, standard and dangerous goods apart (`/api/capacity` `hazmat` list); lines with no size are counted and said.

## Amazon listings and sync

- **One ASIN, several Amazon listings:** a product keeps one SKU + FNSKU, and the FBA sync only refreshes that SKU's FNSKU (an ASIN's other SKUs only fill a blank, or the FNSKU flip-flops). When a new listing is made for the same ASIN (Extra-Body Sculpting Gel and MITCH Pomade, Oct 16) the app kept the old FNSKU. `lib/fnsku-pick.js` (`checkFnskuListings` after each FBA sync / "Sync FNSKUs"): if the saved SKU is gone at Amazon and one other listing is left, it switches by itself; if there are two live listings with different FNSKUs, a red box on Pending Prep and Prepped & Ready asks which one to label with (`/api/fnsku-choose`, `auth`), remembered per ASIN (`inv_cache` `fnsku_ack`) until another listing appears. The check also runs a minute after each start. A FNSKU set by hand (🔧 Fix a wrong FNSKU mapping, `/api/remap-fnsku`) is pinned (`fnsku_pin`) so the sync can't put the old one back, until Amazon shows it under one of the ASIN's listings and the product moves onto that listing (`resolvePins`).

- **Sync with Amazon** (owner 11/8, "taking way too long" and a problem alert every morning): On Hand's button is the QUICK sync (`startAmazonSync('manual')`: shipment check-ins, stock at Amazon and on the way, package sizes for new listings; about a minute); the 6:30 AM sync (`'daily'`) and Admin → App status → "▶ Run the full sync now" (`'full'`, `POST /api/amazon-sync/start {full: true}`) also read every report (restock, sales, returns, reimbursements, stuck stock, refunds/fees, settlements; 10+ minutes). `result.quick` says which ran. Package sizes: `pullItemSizes(all, onStep, { skipRecent })` asks a listing again only a week after the last try (`inv_products.cuft_checked_at`, `inv_asin_sizes.at`) when the sync runs it (the Get sizes button asks everything missing); a listing Amazon has no size for is a `result.notes` line (shown on the Morning sync card and On Hand's last-sync line), not a problem; only an Amazon refusal is a problem (it alerted as "package sizes: 4 failed" every morning for the same four listings).

- **Inventory Value** (owner 11/8, "taking forever"): `getMyPrices` prices 20 ASINs a call with Product Pricing v0 `getPricing` (our own listing price, `lib/prices.js` `myPricesFromPayload`, 0.5 calls/s) and sends only the listings it couldn't price to the one-at-a-time Buy Box lookup (`getOfferPrices`, the old way for everything: 2 s each); the full morning sync also runs the value pull (`result.valued`), so the figure is fresh without the button. The pull covers every listing at Amazon, in the catalog or not (owner 11/8, "missing a ton of items": `outside` rows named from the FBA pull), carries each one's units at Amazon and on the way (`fba_units`, `fba_inbound`), and the tab shows In transit, At Amazon, Value at Amazon and Value everywhere (warehouse + in transit + at Amazon) beside the warehouse figures; the Admin age tags are redrawn when the pull finishes (`loadFreshness`; the tag read 22d after a refresh).

## Look and layout

- **Readability rules (owner 11/5: "max readability and still keep our important info"):** one colour per meaning everywhere — warehouse teal (`#0f766e` family: on the shelf, pending prep, prepped), Amazon blue (`#1d64c8` family: on the way, at Amazon), sales purple (`#6d3fc0`); red / amber / green ONLY for status. Numbers sit in shaded column groups with a labelled band over them (SALES purple · AT AMAZON blue · HEALTH grey; thin lines between groups): On Hand's "Sells as" (`.sa`), On Hand's table (WAREHOUSE · AMAZON · EVERYTHING · SALES & HEALTH, plus a sticky product column, sortable headers via `ohTblSort`, Roomy / Compact `ohDensity`), the Pending Prep / Prepped sell strip (`sellStrip`: `.ss-g` groups), Prepped's Pre-labeled table (`.pgrp`) and Pending Prep's List view (`.lgrp`). Days-of-cover cells get a red / amber / green tint (`h-crit` / `h-warn` / `h-good`: under 21, under 45, beyond). Every card leads with a status chip (On Hand `healthChip` + `duoChip`; Pending Prep / Prepped `healthChipFor`: "Low at Amazon · 12d", "Watch · 31d", "Healthy · 265d", "Can build 120 duos"), uses the short name + size (`amzLinkSized`, full title on hover) and puts ASIN / SKU / FNSKU in small tap-to-copy chips (`idLine` / `idLineHtml`; they call `copyText(v)` without the element, since copyText rewrites the element's text). On Pending Prep and Prepped cards they are a fixed stack under the name (`.idStack`: ASIN, FNSKU under it, a real "🖨 Print labels" button under that, each 200px wide), with the location and status chips on their own line above; owner 11/5: a wrapping row put Print labels left on some cards and right on others, "the same spot for every card". A Pending Prep duo card's bottles are a full-width strip under the ID stack (`.grabStrip`: "Grab N of each bottle", one tile per bottle with name, location chip, cases, available, heading and tiles centered; owner 11/5: stacked in the name column the box ran ~250px tall and the duo card dwarfed the singles). The grab strip, sales strip and Start prep are pinned to the bottom of every Pending Prep card (`margin-top:auto`), so they line up in a row across the grid; the qty edit is a quiet "✎ Edit qty" pill. A duo's "Built from" tiles fold to one line ("Parts A 464 · B 0 → can't build ▾", `ohSaToggle`, remembered in `_saOpen`).

## Brand Finder

- **Brand Finder** (owner 11/8: Cosmoprof brands to start selling, "3+ third-party sellers and at least 100 a day"): Admin → Planning → Brand Finder. The Cosmoprof brand list is pasted (the site needs the owner's login and isn't reachable from the server; a "Copy the instruction for Claude in Chrome" button gives the prompt for Claude in Chrome on the owner's computer). `POST /api/brand-finder/start` (`ownerAuth`) runs per brand: Keepa Product Finder (`keepa.findAsins`, `lib/brand-finder.js` `finderSelection`: the brand, COUNT_NEW ≥ the seller minimum, best rank first) → the top N read with `getProducts(..., { buybox: false })` → `scoreBrand`: a listing is OPEN with N+ third-party sellers (Keepa's new-offer count less Amazon when it sells), units a day = Amazon's "bought in past month" ÷ 30 (a floor; none = 0), the brand qualifies when its open listings sell the minimum a day together. Saved brand by brand in `inv_cache` `brand_finder` (a stop keeps what's done; brands checked in the last 7 days with the same settings are skipped unless Re-check all); brands we already sell are tagged from the catalog's Keepa brand names (`market_data`). The owner's calls (owner 11/8, "I need to filter more … a check mark for brands that have potential … remove ones that have no potential"): ✓ moves a brand into a "⭐ Potential" section at the top, ✕ removes it into a folded "Removed" list with Restore, and a removed brand is skipped by later runs (no tokens); `POST /api/brand-finder/mark` (`ownerAuth`, `{brand, mark: 'potential'|'removed'|null}`, `inv_cache` `brand_finder_marks`). Filters on the list, remembered per device (localStorage `bfFilters`): qualifying / not, Amazon (the retailer) selling on the brand's listings or not, the brand selling itself or not, ours vs new brands, a minimum units a day, a name search; Potential always shows. ACCURACY (owner 11/8: "John Paul Mitchell has 4 third-party sellers and isn't popping up … I'm concerned about the other brands we could have left out"): Keepa's brand filter is an exact match, and Cosmoprof uses company names, so each brand is searched under every spelling at once (`brandVariants`: suffixes like Professional(s) / Systems / Inc / Hair Care dropped, a coded first word alone (K18, ACTIIV), the last two words of a long name, a few known families (Paul Mitchell → Tea Tree, Awapuhi Wild Ginger, MITCH), and the owner's own ✎ Amazon name per brand, `aliases` in `brand_finder_marks`); listings are kept when their Amazon brand matches any spelling (`matchesBrand`); a brand not found says which spellings were tried. Units a day fall back to Keepa's `salesRankDrops30` (`rankDrops30`, marked ≈) when there's no badge. The BRAND SELLING ITSELF: the top 8 listings' real sellers from Amazon (`getOfferSellers`, getItemOffers, no tokens), names from Keepa (`getSellerNames`, 1 token each, cached for good in `inv_cache` `seller_names`), a seller whose name matches the brand is the brand's store (`brandSellers`); on those listings resellers are counted exactly, leaving out Amazon and the brand's store. RETAIL PRICE (owner 11/9, "add retail prices so I can filter out low cost ones"): each listing's price (`retailPrice`) is the Buy Box from Amazon's offers when read (`getOfferSellers` `bbPrice`, listing + shipping), else Keepa's Buy Box, lowest FBA offer (`newFbaPrice`, csv 10), lowest new (`newPrice`) or Amazon's; a brand shows its open listings' median price and the range (`medianPrice`, `minPrice`, `maxPrice`). The "Listings $X and up" filter (`_bfF.minPrice`) recounts every brand in the browser without its listings under that price (`bfPriceCut`, the same as `priceCut` in lib; a listing with no price is kept), so a brand of cheap items stops qualifying; every listing read is saved (up to 100) for that. Results saved before prices were added are checked again on the next run. A brand's listings (tap the brand) have an ASIN column, each a tap-to-copy chip, and "📋 Copy all N ASINs" (one per line; with a price floor set also "Copy the N at $X and up") over the table (owner 11/12, "need asins here for each listing, copyable").
